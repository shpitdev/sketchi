# Artwork attribution

Product marks identify their products. A brand owner's removal request is honored by deleting the mark and its hand-drawn recipe.

## Jenkins

The [Jenkins artwork](https://www.jenkins.io/artwork/) was created by Frontside; Charles Lowell championed the original design. The [source SVG](https://www.jenkins.io/images/logos/jenkins/jenkins.svg) is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

`svg/devtools-ci/jenkins.svg` is a compacted copy. `recipes/jenkins.json` is Sketchi's simplified hand-drawn adaptation. Both retain the CC-BY-SA 3.0 license, separately from the surrounding software's license. No endorsement is implied.
