A flowchart or state-machine line no longer runs through another line's arrowhead, where that reads as pointing the line into the head's target. When a layout still draws one, the lines through a head are routed again after every other line, clear of arrowheads first, and in a graph of up to 24 edges every line is; the clearer layout is kept when it draws no more crossings, reader findings or crowded labels and no longer a drawing.
