Databricks product icons (Unity Catalog, Delta Lake, Databricks SQL, Lakeflow, MLflow and about 60 more) and Snowflake's product and reference-architecture icons (warehouses, dynamic and Iceberg tables, Snowpark, Cortex, roles, policies) are in the catalog and draw by hand, Snowflake's in its brand colors. A label names one only when it names the product ("Unity Catalog", "Snowflake dynamic table"); plain words such as "Notebooks", "Workspace" or "Task" still draw nothing. The CLI now carries its bundled node-logo SVGs compressed, so the package stays small as the catalog grows.
