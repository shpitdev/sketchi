Google Cloud's per-product icons (BigQuery's console set, Pub/Sub, Dataflow, Cloud Functions and about 200 more) are now the "Google Cloud Products" collection and draw by hand in Google's colors, in a new Google Cloud pack. A product in both of Google's sets ships once, as its current icon; its other slug still resolves to it. A node labelled with one everyday word such as "Batch" or "Home" no longer takes the Google Cloud mark of that name.
