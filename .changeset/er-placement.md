ER diagrams of larger schemas read cleaner. Tables move between columns while that leaves fewer crossings, so a hub such as an organizations table sits among its children with links on both sides. Two relationship ends on one side of a row now stand far enough apart that their bars and feet no longer run together, lines no longer wrap around their own table to reach a neighbor, and small schemas stack their tables instead of running out to a wide strip.
