A flowchart whose groups run in one row, or one column, too long to read now lays its groups out in rows, or columns, of whole groups. In expense approvals drawn left to right across Employee, Manager and Finance groups, the three groups used to run in one strip about 4 to 5 times wider than tall; now Employee and Manager share the first row and Finance sits just under Manager, clear of the taller Employee group beside it, about 1.8 to 2 times wider than tall. Each group keeps its frame, name and members together. The line from one row to the next steps down through the gap between the rows, never around the drawing's edge. A wrap is kept only where the drawing has no more crossings, detours or reader findings, port jogs included, than the single row. State machines and diagrams under the length threshold draw as before.
