Four routing fixes. A line that returns to a decision now rises into the decision's nearest free corner instead of winding round to its far side: in an expense flow, the receipt request's return used to go right, up and back left into the receipt check's far face, beside its "Attached" branch; it now turns once into the check's bottom corner. A line between two neighboring rows that has to step sideways across a gap too short for the step to clear both of its ends now gets a taller gap, so the step sits midway instead of jogging just before the arrowhead: in incident response flows, the line from monitoring to the resolution used to kink right above the arrowhead. And a hybrid diagram whose cloud region, wrapped beside the on-prem data center, would cross two lines once no longer stacks the data center above the cloud when moving one line's end to another side removes that crossing: the data center now stays to the left of the cloud. A line whose start sits just past the span of its target's facing side, with a free corridor between, now slides its start along its side and runs straight in: in hybrid diagrams, the sync function's line to the invoice archive used to rise over the archive's corner and hook down into its top.
