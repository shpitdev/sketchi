A top-to-bottom flowchart that is still taller than 2.3:1 and cannot wrap draws its rank gaps tighter. A gap that lines only cross straight shrinks from 96 to 56 units; a gap that lines turn in shrinks to the stubs either side of each turn. This applies to the common shape where a decision's failure branch skips down to a shared end. The tighter layout is kept only when it routes no worse and no line gets longer, hooks or detours. A single-row timeline wider than 2.3:1 stands its labels farther out on longer stems instead of drawing a wide, shallow strip.
