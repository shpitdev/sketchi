A long flowchart that wraps into rows or columns now carries its flow from one band to the next through the gap between them: the line leaves the band's last step by the side facing the next band, runs along the gap and steps into the next band's first step, instead of running around both outer margins. A decision opening the next band is entered by its side vertex, its branches leaving by the other two. Where that route would add a crossing or a reader finding, the line runs around the end of the band it leaves, or around both ends as before. A transition or edge label that only a frame crowds, rather than another line, no longer slides out past the drawing and widens it.
