Flowcharts and state machines draw fewer crossing lines: where lines still cross after layout, a line may move to another side of its node, a loop to the other side of the flow, or a decision's branches to other vertices, whenever that removes a crossing without adding other defects. Lines also keep a clear gap from every node they pass, rounded and pointed ones included, and a transition's label sits clearly nearer its own line than any other.
