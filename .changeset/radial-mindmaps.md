Mindmaps now draw as mindmaps: the central topic sits in the middle as an ellipse, main branches balance on both sides in clockwise order on an arc around it, and each branch's subtree grows outward in its own color, joined by curves that never cross or run through a topic. Label size steps down from the center, and a map too wide to read folds its deepest topics into lists below them. In Excalidraw each curve stays a curved arrow bound to both topics, and each main branch moves as one group.
